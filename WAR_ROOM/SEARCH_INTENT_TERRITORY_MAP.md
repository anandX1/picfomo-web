# Search Intent Territory Map

## 1. Problem Unaware (Aesthetic & Trend Discovery)
* **Emotional State:** Boredom, Vanity, Curiosity.
* **SERP Behavior:** Infinite scrolling, clicking image carousels, watching Short videos.
* **Queries:** "Old money aesthetic trend", "90s yearbook AI", "What is my aesthetic".
* **Required Trust Elements:** High visual fidelity, social proof (TikTok views/likes).
* **Best Page Type:** `/styles/[category]/[aesthetic]` or `/explore` feed.
* **Visual Strategy:** Autoplaying GIFs, Before/After sliders, TikTok embeds.

## 2. Problem Aware (DIY & Friction Avoidance)
* **Emotional State:** Frustration, Insecurity, Urgency.
* **SERP Behavior:** Skimming listicles, reading "How-to" guides, looking for free hacks.
* **Queries:** "How to take a linkedin photo at home", "fix blurry selfie", "look better in photos".
* **Required Trust Elements:** Empathy, acknowledging the pain point, empirical proof of a better way.
* **Best Page Type:** `/problems/[pain-point]` or `/guides/[guide-name]`.
* **Visual Strategy:** Show a terrible DIY photo next to a PicFomo 20-second generation. "Stop trying to fix bad lighting. Let AI do it."

## 3. Solution Aware (The "AI Headshot" Battleground)
* **Emotional State:** Analytical, Skeptical.
* **SERP Behavior:** Opening 5 tabs, ignoring Sponsored Ads, looking for the "Best" listicle.
* **Queries:** "AI headshot generator", "professional AI portrait maker".
* **Required Trust Elements:** "Identity-Lock" guarantee, 20-Second speed metric, recognizable logos, Trustpilot scores.
* **Best Page Type:** `/use-case/[niche]` and the Homepage.
* **Schema Type:** `SoftwareApplication`, `AggregateRating`.

## 4. Comparison Aware (The Transactional Threshold)
* **Emotional State:** Risk-averse, highly analytical.
* **SERP Behavior:** Scanning tables, reading Reddit threads, looking for refund policies.
* **Queries:** "Aragon vs PicFomo", "HeadshotPro alternatives".
* **Required Trust Elements:** Factual, non-biased comparison tables. Exact time metrics. 
* **Best Page Type:** `/compare/[picfomo]-vs-[competitor]`.
* **Visual Strategy:** Side-by-side output comparisons showing competitor's "waxy" skin vs PicFomo's photorealism.

## 5. Trust Validation (The Reddit/Cynic Defense)
* **Emotional State:** Highly skeptical, looking for a reason *not* to buy.
* **SERP Behavior:** Adding "reddit" or "review" to queries. Reading 1-star reviews.
* **Queries:** "Is PicFomo legit", "Do AI headshots look fake reddit".
* **Required Trust Elements:** UGC, raw/unedited community output, technical transparency.
* **Best Page Type:** `/glossary/identity-lock` (Technical proof) and `/reddit/` (Curated community sentiment hub).

## 6. Transaction Ready (The Final Click)
* **Emotional State:** Impatient, ready to close the loop.
* **SERP Behavior:** Direct brand searches, looking for coupons.
* **Queries:** "PicFomo pricing", "PicFomo login".
* **Required Trust Elements:** Secure checkout signals, instant gratification guarantee.
* **Best Page Type:** Pricing/Checkout flow.

## 7. Career/Social Urgency (Micro-Intents)
* **Emotional State:** Panic, Status anxiety.
* **Queries:** "need a headshot by tomorrow", "tinder profile upgrade".
* **PicFomo Advantage:** This is where the 20-second generation time mathematically wins the market. No other tool can satisfy this emotional urgency.
