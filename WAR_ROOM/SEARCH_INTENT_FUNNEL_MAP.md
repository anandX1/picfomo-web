# Search Intent Funnel Map

## 1. The Funnel Architecture

### Stage 1: Problem-Unaware (The Inspiration Phase)
* **Psychology:** The user isn't looking for a headshot; they are looking at trends or seeking aesthetic inspiration.
* **Queries:** "old money aesthetic", "90s yearbook trend", "cinematic lighting portrait"
* **SERP Format:** Image packs, TikTok videos, Pinterest boards.
* **PicFomo Strategy:** Capture via social media sharing of the `/explore` feed and `/styles` pages.

### Stage 2: Problem-Aware (The Friction Phase)
* **Psychology:** The user needs a photo but hates the traditional process. They are looking for workarounds.
* **Queries:** "how to take a good headshot at home", "diy linkedin photo", "fix blurry selfie"
* **SERP Format:** YouTube tutorials, blog posts, step-by-step guides.
* **PicFomo Strategy:** Blog content (`/guides/perfect-selfie-for-ai-headshots`) that pivots the user from "DIY photography" to "Instant AI Generation".

### Stage 3: Solution-Aware (The Discovery Phase)
* **Psychology:** The user knows AI headshots exist but doesn't know which tool to use.
* **Queries:** "ai headshot generator", "ai portrait creator", "make my photo look professional ai"
* **SERP Format:** Listicles, sponsored ads, tool directories.
* **PicFomo Strategy:** Rank the homepage (`/`) and specialized use-case pages (`/use-case/linkedin-profile-pictures`). Highlight speed ("20 seconds") and quality ("Identity-Lock").

### Stage 4: Comparison-Aware (The Evaluation Phase)
* **Psychology:** The user has found 2-3 tools and is trying to avoid getting scammed or receiving "waxy" results.
* **Queries:** "aragon ai vs headshotpro", "best ai headshot reddit", "is tryiton ai worth it"
* **SERP Format:** Reddit threads, comparison tables, YouTube reviews.
* **PicFomo Strategy:** Rank the `/compare` pages. Present objective, empirical data (Speed: 20s vs 2 hours) to win the logical argument.

### Stage 5: Purchase-Intent (The Transactional Phase)
* **Psychology:** The user is ready to pull the trigger but is checking for discounts or safety guarantees.
* **Queries:** "picfomo pricing", "picfomo promo code", "picfomo refund"
* **SERP Format:** Brand-specific pages.
* **PicFomo Strategy:** Clear pricing pages, strong FAQ structured data, and high-trust "Money Back" or "Free Trial" signals.

### Stage 6: Showcase/Social-Sharing Intent (The Viral Phase)
* **Psychology:** The user loved the result and wants validation from their network.
* **Queries:** N/A (Direct sharing behavior).
* **PicFomo Strategy:** The `/post/[id]` pages. When shared on Twitter/LinkedIn, the OpenGraph metadata must look stunning to pull Stage 1 users back into the funnel.

## 2. Strategic Gaps in the Market
Most competitors focus 90% of their SEO on Stage 3 ("ai headshot generator"). 
**PicFomo Opportunity:** Dominate Stage 2 (DIY pivots) and Stage 4 (Aggressive, factual comparison pages). Furthermore, expanding the programmatic Use Cases captures highly specific Stage 3 long-tail traffic that competitors ignore (e.g., "AI headshots for travel nurses").
