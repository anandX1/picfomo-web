# Human Search Behavior Report

## 1. Core Psychological Drivers
Users searching for "AI headshots" or "AI portraits" are not looking for software; they are looking for **status, validation, and professional safety**.

* **The Insecurity Trigger:** Most users hate having their photo taken. The search is driven by the desire to bypass the vulnerability of a photoshoot. They want the result (looking successful/attractive) without the process (posing, lighting, feeling judged).
* **The Cost-Aversion Trigger:** Professional photography is perceived as expensive and time-consuming. Queries like "cheap AI headshots" or "free AI portrait generator" are rooted in budget anxiety.
* **The Aspirational Trigger:** Users are searching for an upgraded version of themselves. They are looking to match the perceived status of their peers on LinkedIn or dating apps. 

## 2. Search Contexts & Urgency
Search queries in this niche are highly contextual and often driven by sudden urgency:
* **The "Job Hunt" Panic:** The user just updated their resume, went to update LinkedIn, and realized their current photo is 5 years old. (High urgency, low patience).
* **The "Dating App" Refresh:** The user is frustrated with match rates and seeks a quick, low-effort upgrade. (High vanity, aesthetic focus).
* **The "Company Directory" Mandate:** The user was just told by HR to provide a professional headshot by Friday. (Compliance driven, seeks "corporate" safety).

## 3. Social & Community Signals (Reddit, Quora, TikTok)
* **Reddit (Skepticism & Realism):** Subreddits like r/resumes and r/artificial are highly skeptical of "AI waxy skin" and "weird eyes". They search for "AI headshots that don't look fake reddit". *Insight:* Authenticity and the absence of AI artifacts are the highest currency here.
* **TikTok (Trend & Transformation):** The behavior here is voyeuristic. Users engage with "Before/After" slider videos. They want to see the magic trick. *Insight:* Viral hooks require dramatic, instant transformations.
* **LinkedIn (Professional Acceptability):** Conversations revolve around whether AI headshots are "lying" or acceptable. *Insight:* The product must position itself as a "digital enhancement," not a "fake identity."

## 4. Trust Mechanics in Human Search
When users land on a page from a search, they evaluate trust within 3 seconds based on:
1. **The "Uncanny Valley" Check:** They instantly scan the hero images for crossed eyes, melted teeth, or plastic skin.
2. **The "Real Human" Proxy:** They look for UGC (User Generated Content) showing a messy original selfie transformed into a great output.
3. **The Refund/Risk Check:** Because AI tools historically fail on edge cases (glasses, specific ethnicities), users actively search for "Aragon AI refund policy" before buying.

## 5. Strategic Takeaways for PicFomo
* **Positioning:** Sell "Identity-Lock™" as the antidote to the "fake/waxy" fear prevalent on Reddit.
* **Speed as a Status Symbol:** For the panicked job seeker, "20 seconds" is a massive conversion trigger compared to the competitor's "2 hour" wait time.
* **Aspirational Alignment:** Visuals must reflect where the user *wants* to be (luxury, success), not just a generic gray background.
