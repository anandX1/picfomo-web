* **Weakly Defended (Opportunities):**

## 5. Strategic Takeaway
Shift SEO focus from head-terms to the intersections of entities. E.g., `Context + Modifier + Output` -> "Instant LinkedIn Headshots without a photoshoot". Connect these concepts via internal linking to build an impenetrable semantic graph.
