# Search Ecosystem Map

## 1. The Asymmetric Intent Flow
The AI portrait ecosystem is not a linear funnel; it is a chaotic, multi-platform dependency graph. A user does not simply "search and buy." They bounce between platforms to satisfy different psychological checkpoints (Inspiration → Skepticism → Validation → Transaction).

### A. The Inspiration & Urgency Nodes (Where the cycle starts)
* **LinkedIn (Status Threat):** A user scrolls, sees a peer's stunning new headshot, and feels immediate status anxiety. *Intent:* Aspirational.
* **TikTok / Pinterest (Aesthetic Discovery):** A user sees a "90s Yearbook" or "Old Money" transformation video. *Intent:* Voyeuristic / Entertainment.
* **Slack / HR Email (Compliance Threat):** "Please upload your headshot to the new directory by Friday." *Intent:* High urgency, low friction desired.

### B. The Navigation & Evaluation Nodes (The Search)
* **Google (The Evaluation Switchboard):** Users don't trust Google for *truth*; they use it for *options*. Queries here ("best AI headshot generator") are meant to gather candidates.
* **ChatGPT / Perplexity (The Curator):** Users ask, "Which AI headshot generator actually looks real and isn't a scam?" LLMs act as trust filters, cutting out the SEO noise.

### C. The Validation Nodes (Where trust is forged or broken)
* **Reddit / Quora (The Cynic's Sandbox):** *Crucial node.* Users take the candidates from Google/LLMs and append "reddit" to the query (e.g., "Aragon AI vs TryItOn reddit"). They are actively looking for reasons *not* to buy (waxy skin, scams, no refunds).
* **Review Sites (Trustpilot):** Used as a secondary risk-mitigation step. Users look specifically for 1-star reviews to see worst-case scenarios.

## 2. The Conversion Nexus
Where does the actual purchase decision happen?
**Not on the landing page.** The decision happens *in the transition* between Reddit (Validation) and the Landing Page (Transaction). If Reddit says "Aragon is waxy, but PicFomo has Identity-Lock and is instant," the user arrives at PicFomo already pre-closed. The landing page merely facilitates the transaction.

### Ecosystem Warfare Strategy for PicFomo:
1. **Intercept at Inspiration:** Use TikTok/LinkedIn to plant the seed. OpenGraph tags on shared posts must incite status anxiety ("Wait, that's an AI photo?").
2. **Dominate the Validation Node:** PicFomo must own the Reddit narrative. A single highly upvoted post saying "PicFomo is the only one that didn't mess up my eyes" is worth 100,000 organic Google visits.
3. **Capture the Transaction:** By the time they hit Google for "PicFomo pricing," the battle is already won.
